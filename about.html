About

Graphic designer in Nairobi, working with organizations.

Clear pictures and few words

I am a graphic designer in Nairobi. I work mostly with organizations: NGOs, community groups, schools and events.

I mainly design posters, print and social media content. More recently I have taken on magazines and reports, long documents that still need to hold attention on every page. Brand design is the newest part of my work.

I work in Canva and Affinity, and I am learning the Adobe suite. My designs combine real photography, layered collage, bold type and texture, with one clear message on every piece.

How I design

Four habits

  • Contrast

    Large against small, bright against dark, picture against open space.

  • Hierarchy

    The eye should know where to start before the reader decides to read.

  • Texture

    Grain, paper and glitch effects give flat colour some depth.

  • Restraint

    Few words and one clear message. A single strong picture beats five weak ones.
